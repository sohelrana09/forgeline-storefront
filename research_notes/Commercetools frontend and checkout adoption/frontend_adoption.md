# commercetools Frontend (formerly Frontastic) vs custom frontends: adoption and direction

Research date: 2026-10-30. Labels: **[FACT]** = stated by a primary or named source; **[VENDOR CLAIM]** = marketing figure from commercetools or a competitor; **[ESTIMATE]** = third-party estimate or my inference. About 20 tool calls; several questions remain open (see Gaps).

## 1. Total customers, and how many use commercetools Frontend

### Takeaway
commercetools now cites "more than 600 brands" (July 2026 boilerplate) and "500+ enterprises" (site call to action). It publishes **no count or share of Frontend customers**. The publicly named Frontend users I found number about 10 mid-market brands, mostly European, mostly 2021-2023. The flagship enterprise references are not shown as Frontend users.

### Cited Findings
- [VENDOR CLAIM, 2026-07-15] The About boilerplate says "more than 600 global brands" rely on commercetools, with over $110B annualized GMV, over 700M orders and more than 1B customer records. The same page's footer says "500+ enterprises", so the two figures disagree. — [commercetools press release, Modular Commerce Offerings](https://commercetools.com/press-releases/commercetools-launches-modular-commerce-offerings)
- [VENDOR CLAIM, 2026-04-22] Q1 2026: a $100B GMV run rate, over 600M orders a year (+41% YoY), same-store sales +27% YoY, more than $7B GMV from new enterprise customers, 36 countries. No customer count is given. — [PR Newswire, Q1 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/commercetools-reports-strong-q1-2026-growth-surpassing-the-100b-gmv-run-rate-mark-302749651.html)
- [VENDOR CLAIM, 2026] The Frontend product page says "Join 500+ enterprises", which is a platform-wide figure, not a Frontend one. — [commercetools Frontend product page](https://commercetools.com/products/frontend)
- [ESTIMATE, third party, undated] ReadyContacts claims "over 350 enterprise customers" (J&J, Carhartt, Yamaha Motors, The North Face, L'Oréal). Its method is unclear. — [ReadyContacts](https://www.readycontacts.com/target-account-profiling/commercetools/)
- [FACT, 2023-01] Named Frontend users at the GA launch were flaconi, Lakrids by Bülow, Tom Tailor and CHRONEXT. — [commercetools press release, Frontend launch](https://commercetools.com/press-releases/commercetools-launches-commercetools-frontend-to-help-businesses-take-advantage-of-a-composable-tech-without-sacrificing-time-to-market); [Enterprise Times, 2023-01-18](https://www.enterprisetimes.co.uk/2023/01/18/commercetools-frontend-launched-to-help-businesses-with-composable-technology/)
- [FACT, 2026 page] Logos currently on the Frontend product page: Christie Cookie Co. (+47% AOV YoY), Chronext (300% faster site, +10% conversion), flaconi (300 peak orders per minute), APG & Co, BIC, Loomstate. — [commercetools Frontend product page](https://commercetools.com/products/frontend)
- [FACT, 2021-11] Before the acquisition, Frontastic was used by Universal Music, flaconi and APG & Co. — [commercetools press release, Frontastic acquisition](https://commercetools.com/press-releases/commercetools-acquires-the-leading-composable-frontend-platform-frontastic)

### Inferences
- [ESTIMATE] The named Frontend reference base (about 10 brands, the same names since 2021-2023) is small against 500 to 600+ platform customers. This suggests a **minority share, likely well under 20%**. That is my inference, not a sourced figure.
- The large enterprise references (Audi, Danone, Sephora, Volkswagen, NBCUniversal, Ulta, Lululemon) are not shown as Frontend users on the Frontend page.

### Gaps
- No official or analyst figure for the number or percentage of Frontend customers was found.
- No figure for Frontastic's customer count at the time of the acquisition was found.

## 2. What happened to Frontastic after the 2021 acquisition (2024-2026 status)

### Takeaway
The timeline runs Frontastic (acquired November 2021), then commercetools Frontend (GA January 2023), then 2024-2026 maintenance releases (Store Launchpads 2.0.0, January 2026). The product is still sold, but company messaging in 2025-2026 has moved to "the Digital Commerce AI Company": AgentSphere, Stripe agentic commerce, **commercetools for Builders** (AI coding tools such as Claude Code, Cursor and Vercel v0 generating storefronts, June 2026) and **standalone Core Commerce and Product Catalog modules** (July 2026). None of these 2026 launch releases mention commercetools Frontend. I found no deprecation or end-of-sale notice.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT, 2021-11] commercetools acquired Frontastic and called it "the first of a new portfolio strategy". — [commercetools blog](https://commercetools.com/blog/commercetools-buys-frontastic-the-first-of-a-new-portfolio-strategy); [Techzine](https://www.techzine.eu/news/devops/68648/commercetools-acquires-front-and-backend-development-tool-frontastic/)
- [FACT, 2023-01] The product was rebranded and launched as commercetools Frontend. — [Enterprise Times](https://www.enterprisetimes.co.uk/2023/01/18/commercetools-frontend-launched-to-help-businesses-with-composable-technology/)
- [FACT per search summary of docs, 2026-01] The developer docs list B2C Retail Store Launchpad 2.0.0 and B2B Manufacturing Launchpad 2.0.0 (January 2026), a Figma UI kit for the B2B launchpad, and Node.js 20 unsupported from 2026-04-30. I could not verify this in full text, because the releases page did not render on fetch. — [Frontend Development Releases](https://docs.commercetools.com/frontend-development/releases); [Release notes, Frontend filter](https://docs.commercetools.com/docs/release-notes?product=Frontend&tab=product)
- [FACT, 2026] The Frontend product page is live with "Start free trial" and "Contact us" calls to action and no end-of-sale notice. It is now positioned under "Digital Commerce" and links to "Build with AI" (commercetools for Builders). Studio, launchpads and hosting are no longer named on the page. — [commercetools Frontend product page](https://commercetools.com/products/frontend)
- [FACT, 2025-02-26] commercetools laid off about 10% of staff (CEO Andrew Burton). An anonymous source put it at more than 70 people and up to 20% including earlier cuts, which the company disputes. The CRO and CFO left the executive team. The article does not mention Frontend. ARR was "well above $100M" as of July 2024. Valuation was $1.9B in 2021. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/26/commercetools-a-pioneer-in-headless-commerce-lays-off-dozens-of-staff); [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/02/27/commercetools-layoffs-restructurings/)
- [FACT] Andrew Burton became CEO on 2024-07-08, succeeding Dirk Hoerig, who became Chief Innovation Officer. — [commercetools press release](https://commercetools.com/press-releases/welcoming-andrew-burton-as-our-new-ceo)
- [FACT, 2026] Doug McNary is CEO in the April and July 2026 releases. I did not find the date he was appointed. — [Q1 2026 release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/commercetools-reports-strong-q1-2026-growth-surpassing-the-100b-gmv-run-rate-mark-302749651.html); [Modular offerings release](https://commercetools.com/press-releases/commercetools-launches-modular-commerce-offerings)
- [FACT, 2026-06-23] "commercetools for Builders" (skills libraries, prompts, AI development workflows) lets AI coding tools generate storefronts, catalogs and checkout, "from B2C storefronts to B2B ordering portals". It works with Claude Code, v0 and Cursor. Vercel's Head of v0, Fernando Rojo, is quoted. A Commerce Integration Layer is due later in 2026. The release does not mention commercetools Frontend. — [commercetools press release, Builders](https://commercetools.com/press-releases/commercetools-reduces-enterprise-commerce-delivery)
- [FACT, 2026-07-15] Core Commerce (cart, order, checkout, customer, B2B) and Product Catalog (modeling, pricing, inventory, search) are sold as standalone modules on "Sphere" for new customers. The release says they "integrate with existing technology investments" and does not mention a frontend. — [commercetools press release](https://commercetools.com/press-releases/commercetools-launches-modular-commerce-offerings)
- [FACT, 2026-01-12] At NRF 2026: Stripe Agentic Commerce Suite, AI Hub, Nespresso as a new customer, JD Sports. — [PR Newswire, NRF 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/commercetools-kicks-off-nrf-2026--including-updates-on-stripe-acs-ai-hub-and-helping-enterprises-stay-discoverable-and-shoppable-in-agentic-commerce-302658128.html)
- [FACT, 2026-10-27] ETAM Group is replacing its legacy platform with commercetools, and ETAM's own development team is leading the build. — [Agile Brand Guide news digest](https://agilebrandguide.com/yesterdays-marketing-technology-ai-news-october-28-2026)
- [VENDOR OPINION, competitor, 2026-10] Laioutr, a frontend vendor, argues that the modular move makes "frontend independence" more practical. — [Laioutr blog](https://www.laioutr.com/en/blog/commercetools-standalone-modules-frontend-independence-2026)

### Inferences
- [ESTIMATE] Frontend looks deprioritized in messaging rather than sunset. The 2026 build story is "bring your own frontend, generated with AI tools (v0, Claude Code, Cursor)", which competes with Frontend's original promise of faster time to market. Whether staff cuts touched the Frontend team is not public.
- "commercetools Foundry" did not appear in any source found. Treat it as unverified.

### Gaps
- Frontend pricing and bundling details for 2024-2026 were not found (the pricing page was not fetched).
- No public statement on the Frontend roadmap or end of life was found.
- Partnerships with Netlify, Contentstack, Amplience, Bloomreach and Uniform as frontend alternatives were not researched in the time available.

## 4. Agency, SI and analyst views on frontend choice

### Takeaway
Little public, sourced material turned up. Gartner rated commercetools a Leader in 2022 through 2026 (seventh straight year in 2026). The 2022 coverage cited its "decoupled storefront editor" as a strength. The 2025 and 2026 cautions are gated.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT, 2022] CX Today's summary of the 2022 Gartner MQ lists commercetools' decoupled storefront editor (Frontastic), autoscaling and enterprise support as strengths. — [CX Today](https://www.cxtoday.com/crm/gartner-magic-quadrant-digital-commerce-platforms/)
- [FACT, 2026-10-22] commercetools was named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner MQ for Digital Commerce Platforms, its seventh straight year. — [commercetools press release](https://commercetools.com/press-releases/commercetools-named-a-leader-in-2026-gartner-r-magic-quadrant-tm-for-digital-commerce-as-ai-reshapes-the-market); [Gartner abstract, 2026-10-19](https://www.gartner.com/doc/8518677)
- [FACT] Vercel describes commercetools as having "no storefront of its own" and pitches Next.js on Vercel as the storefront layer. This is competitor framing that ignores Frontend. — [Vercel](https://vercel.com/i/commercetools-alternatives)
- [FACT] Agency rankings list Next.js and React headless builds over commercetools as a standard pairing. — [FactoryJet, Headless Commerce Agencies for Next.js 2026](https://factoryjet.com/blog/best-headless-commerce-agencies-nextjs-2026)

### Gaps
- No MACH Alliance or Forrester survey data on frontend choice was found.
- No public statements from Valtech, Publicis Sapient, Accenture, Netguru, Rangle or Orium were retrieved in the time available.

## 5. Technology-detection data

### Takeaway
BuiltWith detects about 2,432 live commercetools sites, of which 278 are in the US, and about 4,700 including historical ones. Because commercetools is headless, these counts are unreliable and say nothing about frontend frameworks.

### Cited Findings
- [ESTIMATE, BuiltWith, 2026 page] 2,432 live sites, about 2,268 historical (about 4,700 total), 278 US live. The Market Share subpage shows older numbers (1,253 historical, 290 US). — [BuiltWith usage](https://trends.builtwith.com/shop/commercetools); [BuiltWith market share](https://trends.builtwith.com/shop/commercetools/Market-Share)
- [ESTIMATE] The Web Radar detects only 17 companies, which shows how weak detection is for headless platforms. — [The Web Radar](https://the-web-radar.com/technologies/commercetools)

### Inferences
- BuiltWith's 2,400+ "sites" against 600 brands implies several domains per brand (country sites, brands). Detection likely relies on API or asset fingerprints, not the frontend.

### Gaps
- I did not obtain a cross-tab of commercetools sites by frontend framework (Next.js, Nuxt, Frontend) from BuiltWith, Wappalyzer or StoreLeads. It needs paid access.
